Safety margin

Default Alpha floor: 10%.

  • SAFE MARGIN — viable and meets the floor.
  • THIN MARGIN — viable but below the floor.
  • NOT VIABLE — fracture fails.

Objectives

  • Balanced operations: safety → operators → hulls → instability → consumables → margin.
  • Minimum hulls: safety → hulls → operators → margin → instability.
  • Minimum crew: safety → operators → hulls → margin → instability.
  • Maximum margin: safety → margin → hulls → operators → instability.
  • Minimum instability: safety → instability → hulls → operators → margin.
  • Minimum consumables: safety → consumables → hulls → operators → margin.

Fracture evaluation

Effective head power = raw head power × resistance transfer factor
Total effective power = Σ effective head power
Max breakable mass = 5 × total effective power

Objective alternatives

The selected objective produces the highlighted plan. MFA also retains materially different best plans for Balanced, Fewest Hulls, Fewest Operators, Highest Margin, Lowest Instability and Lowest Consumables.
