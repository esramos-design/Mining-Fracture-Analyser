Operational metrics

Hulls
Recommended vessel hull count.
Operators
Prospector/Golem = 1 each; MOLE = pilot + active mining stations.
Mining heads
Total active recommended heads/systems.
Consumables
Active-module uses plus gadget use.

Safety margin

Default Alpha floor: 10%.

  • SAFE MARGIN — viable and meets the floor.
  • THIN MARGIN — viable but below the floor.
  • NOT VIABLE — fracture fails.

Objectives

  • Balanced operations: safety → operators → hulls → instability → consumables → margin.
  • Minimum hulls: safety → hulls → operators → margin → instability.
  • Minimum crew: safety → operators → hulls → margin → instability.
  • Maximum margin: safety → margin → hulls → operators → instability.
  • Minimum instability: safety → instability → hulls → operators → margin.
  • Minimum consumables: safety → consumables → hulls → operators → margin.

Fracture evaluation

Effective head power = raw head power × resistance transfer factor
Total effective power = Σ effective head power
Max breakable mass = 5 × total effective power

Ideal Loadout v2 changes the search and ranking layer only. The audited 4.10.1 fracture engine remains unchanged.

Gadget selection and comparison

With gadgets enabled, MFA evaluates every eligible gadget, including None, against each candidate vessel/head/module plan through the protected fracture engine. The recommended gadget may differ from the one on your actual deployed Fleet Planner.

Expand Gadget comparison beneath the selected plan to see the same fleet evaluated with different gadgets: fracture margin, final resistance and final instability. The selected gadget is highlighted. One gadget is modelled per candidate, not stacked.

Important limitation: charge-rate, charge-window and cluster modifiers are displayed as equipment reference attributes, but are not part of the audited fracture-power/safety-margin calculation. A superior modelled power margin does not guarantee an operationally preferable charge window.

Objective alternatives

The selected objective produces the highlighted plan. MFA also retains materially different best plans for Balanced, Fewest Hulls, Fewest Operators, Highest Margin, Lowest Instability and Lowest Consumables.

Current Alpha boundary

Independent vessel specialization currently searches Primary / Backup A / Backup B deterministic variants. It does not yet exhaust every possible head/module permutation. The search remains deterministic and reproducible.
